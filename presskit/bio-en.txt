DJ Jesse Jay – Biography (English)

Since 1997, DJ Jesse Jay (real name Michael Fellner) has been shaping Zurich's electronic music scene. His sets are soulful journeys that expand horizons and play with sensual fantasies. He loves becoming one with the music and sharing these emotions with the crowd.

Having grown up in legendary clubs like Aera, Labyrinth, SpiderGalaxy, and Dachkantine, he embodies the "we are family" philosophy of Zurich's party scene. Since 2001, he has been running his radio show on Radio LoRa (lora.ch & DAB+), which has been called "The Blue Dimension" since 2025. Every Thursday from midnight to 6 AM, he delivers profound and timeless musical experiences.

Musical styles: Progressive House, Techno, Deep House, Organic House
Origin: Zurich, Switzerland 🇨🇭
