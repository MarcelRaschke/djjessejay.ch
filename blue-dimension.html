APPLICATION RULES
  1. Glow is structural. Never a drop shadow on type — the neon is drawn by the stroke itself.
  2. Two accents only. Cyan carries signal, magenta carries identity. Amber only as a pilot tone.
  3. Everything is a line. Outlines, never fills — the matrix is drawn, not painted.
  4. Dark by default. The hull is the interface; light is the content.
  5. Motion decays. Glow fades as motion slows. Nothing pulses for decoration alone.
  6. Micro-copy carries the system. Frequencies, coordinates, timestamps, hash marks.
